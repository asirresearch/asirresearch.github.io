本教程来源于ASIR 研究组的本科生及研究生同学，在学习深圳元创兴公司提供的相关视频及文字
资料后的总结笔记，由河南工业大学ASIR研究组整理。教程旨在为ASIR研究组内有意参加RAICOM
竞赛的同学提供ROS学习及参赛项目实验参考。

教程内容开源，每年由ASIR研究组同学（在RAICOM竞赛中获得国家级奖项）负责维护更新。

This tutorial originates from the summary notes of undergraduate and graduate students in the ASIR research group, compiled after studying the video and textual materials provided by Shenzhen Yuanchuangxing Co., Ltd. It is organized by the ASIR research group at Henan University of Technology. The tutorial aims to provide a reference for ROS learning and experimental guidance on competition projects for ASIR members who intend to participate in the RAICOM competition.

The tutorial content is open-source and is maintained and updated annually by ASIR students who have won national awards in the RAICOM competition.
